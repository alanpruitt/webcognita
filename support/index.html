Unstarted milestones may be cancelled in writing prior to work commencement for a full refund of any prepaid unexpended balance. Any deliverable non-conformance reported within 15 business days of delivery will be remediated at zero additional cost or credited/refunded in accordance with the SOW.
  • Fast Direct Resolution for P-Card Administrators: Before initiating a bank dispute for an unrecognized descriptor, missing PO code, or tax-exemption adjustment, email alan@webcognita.com with the transaction date and last 4 card digits for priority same-day or next-business-day resolution.
