We use your information solely for legitimate operational and commercial purposes:

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal or business data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers. Data is shared exclusively with trusted infrastructure partners required to deliver our core services:

  • Stripe, Inc. & Wise: Recurring payment processing, merchant checkout, and cross-border currency settlement under strict PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.
  • GitHub Pages: Web hosting, content delivery, and infrastructure distribution.
  • Legal Requirements: When compelled by United States federal or state law, court order, or official regulatory authority.

Webcognita LLC implements robust technical and administrative security controls to protect your data:

  • Encrypted Transport: All website traffic, API connections, and checkout sessions are encrypted end-to-end using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
