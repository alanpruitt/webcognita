Accessibility Statement - Webcognita LLC

Accessibility Conformance Statement

Webcognita LLC is dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including individuals with disabilities. We build, audit, and refactor our web platforms and client curriculum architectures to conform with WCAG 2.2 Level AA standards and the federal ADA Title II Final Rule (28 CFR Part 35).

Conformance Status

  • Standard: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.
  • Current Status: Fully Conforming. All service pages, evaluation matrices, contact pathways, and documentation meet or exceed Level AA standards.
  • Automated CI/CD Validation: Every deployment is tested against headless browser suites with Axe-core and Playwright prior to production release.

Accessibility Measures

  • Semantic Structure: Headings follow a strict hierarchy beginning at <h2> for section bodies, with <h1> reserved exclusively for page titles.
  • Contrast Compliance: All text and interactive elements maintain a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text and 3:1 for large text.
  • Touch Targets & Navigation: Interactive controls provide minimum 44 × 44px target perimeters with visible :focus-visible styling for keyboard and screen reader users.
