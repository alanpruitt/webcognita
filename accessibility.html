Accessibility Statement - Webcognita LLC

Accessibility Conformance Statement

Webcognita LLC (founded and led by Alan Pruitt, USMC Veteran & Google Gemini Certified Faculty) is dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including individuals with disabilities. Webcognita builds, audits, and refactors web platforms and client Curriculum-as-Code architectures to conform with WCAG 2.2 Level AA (enforcing a 9:1 Double-Contrast Guard), WCAG 3.0 APCA perceptual contrast (100,000+ lux), and the federal ADA Title II Final Rule (28 CFR Part 35).

Conformance Status

  • Standard: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA (9:1 Contrast Guard) & WCAG 3.0 APCA / Cognitive Accessibility (*In Plain English* / *En palabras sencillas*).
  • Current Status: Fully Conforming. All service pages, evaluation matrices, contact pathways, and documentation meet or exceed Level AA standards.
  • Compliance-as-Code CI/CD Validation: Every deployment is tested against headless browser suites with Axe-core, Playwright, and Argon 4 prior to production release.

Accessibility Measures

  • Semantic Structure: Headings follow a strict hierarchy beginning at <h2> for section bodies, with <h1> reserved exclusively for page titles.
  • 9:1 Double-Contrast Guard: Exceeding the 4.5:1 WCAG 2.2 AA minimum, all primary text and interactive elements enforce a minimum 9:1 contrast ratio and zero decorative Unicode emojis for screen-reader clarity.
  • Touch Targets & Bilingual Parity: Interactive controls provide minimum 44 × 44px target perimeters with visible :focus-visible styling and W3C SKOS English / Norteño-Spanish screen-reader parity.
